What this does and doesn't show
  • From the STEP export: link lengths, the level-keeping and crank parallelograms, the claw body and its offset from the wrist pin (84.3 mm down, 15.7 mm forward), the deck (top 39.5 mm below the shoulder axis, front edge 75 mm ahead), the servo bracket, and the claw gear ratio (24-tooth, module 0.7 pinion: 0.29 mm of gap per servo degree).
  • Measured: shoulder axis 152.4 mm (about 6 in) above the floor. The CAD has 160.2 mm.
  • Assumed until calibrated (CAL in the firmware): 10 µs per servo degree with opposite signs on the two MG996Rs (they face each other), horns fitted so 1500 µs gives θ1 = 45° and θ2 = −30°, claw gap 30 mm at 1500 µs, and the 20°/160° fold limits from the CAD notes.
  • Not modelled: servo speed under load, play in printed joints, grip friction and power supply sag. Those still need bench tests. The wheels sit beside the arm, so they are drawn faint and not checked. The jaws open sideways, so the side view shows the near finger and the inset shows the gap.
